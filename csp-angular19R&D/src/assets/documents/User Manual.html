1. Getting Started

Purpose: This section guides you through accessing the neurealm Customer Success Management Platform, logging in for the first time, and understanding system requirements. The platform provides comprehensive tools for managing customer success, tracking KPIs, and maintaining quality standards across all customer engagements.

1.1 System Requirements

Supported Browsers
The neurealm CSM platform works best with the following browsers:
  • Google Chrome (version 90 or higher) — Recommended
  • Microsoft Edge (version 90 or higher)
  • Mozilla Firefox (version 88 or higher)
  • Safari (version 14 or higher)
Ensure JavaScript is enabled and popup blockers allow neurealm.com.

1.2 Accessing the Platform

  1. Open your web browser
    Launch one of the supported browsers listed above
  2. Navigate to the platform URL
    Enter the neurealm CSM platform URL provided by your system administrator
  3. Bookmark the page
    Save the URL to your bookmarks for easy access (Press Ctrl+D on Windows or Cmd+D on Mac)
Figure 1.1: neurealm CSM Login Page

1.3 Logging In with Username & Password

  1. Enter your username
    Type your assigned username in the "Username" field
  2. Enter your password
    Type your password in the "Password" field (password characters are hidden for security)
  3. Click "Sign In" button
    Click the blue "Sign In" button to proceed to the dashboard
Field Name Description Validation Rules
Username * Your unique username assigned by the system administrator Required, case-sensitive
Password * Your secure password Minimum 8 characters, case-sensitive

1.4 Microsoft 365 Authentication (Alternative Login)

  1. Click "Sign in with Microsoft" button
    Available on the login page below the standard login form
  2. Select your Microsoft 365 account
    Choose the Microsoft 365 account associated with your neurealm access
  3. Authorize access
    Click "Allow" to grant neurealm CSM access to your profile information
  4. Redirected to landing page
    You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the appropriate dashboard
Quick Tip: OAuth Benefits
Microsoft 365 login is faster and more secure. It eliminates the need to remember separate passwords and leverages your existing Microsoft 365 account security features including multi-factor authentication.

1.5 First-Time Login & Password Reset

Figure 1.2: Forgot Password Page
  1. Click "Forgot Password?" link
    Located below the login form on the main login page
  2. Enter your email address
    Type the email address associated with your account
  3. Click "Send Reset Link" button
    A password reset link will be sent to your email within 2-3 minutes
  4. Check your email inbox
    Open the email from neurealm CSM (check spam/junk folder if not found)
  5. Click the reset link
    The link will take you to the password reset page (valid for 24 hours)
  6. Enter new password
    Type your new password meeting the security requirements below
  7. Confirm new password
    Re-type your new password to confirm it matches
  8. Click "Reset Password" button
    Your password will be updated and you can now log in with the new credentials
Password Requirements
Your password must meet ALL of the following criteria:
  • Minimum 8 characters long
  • Include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • Include at least one lowercase letter (a-z)
  • Include at least one number (0-9)
  • Include at least one special character (@, #, $, %, !, etc.)
  • Cannot be the same as your username

SMART KPIs
Configure KPIs following SMART criteria:
  • Specific — Clearly defined metric with no ambiguity
  • Measurable — Quantifiable with defined measurement approach
  • Achievable — Realistic targets based on baseline and resources
  • Relevant — Directly tied to customer goals and business outcomes
  • Time-bound — Clear timeframe for achievement with reporting frequency
Target Setting Best Practices
Set realistic yet challenging targets. Overly aggressive targets can demotivate teams and damage credibility with customers. Review baseline performance, industry benchmarks, and available resources when setting target values. Targets should stretch capabilities but remain achievable with proper effort and execution.

Overdue Action Items
Action items marked "Over Due" or labeled "Due for closure" require immediate attention. Consistently overdue action items can negatively impact customer health scores and project performance. Review overdue items daily and either complete them, reassign them, or adjust due dates with appropriate justification.
Action Item Best Practices
  • SMART Action Items: Ensure each action item is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound
  • Clear Ownership: Always assign a single owner (avoid "unassigned" or shared ownership)
  • Link to Source: Connect action items to originating risks, issues, or customer requests for traceability
  • Regular Updates: Update status and add comments at least weekly, even if minimal progress
  • Completion Notes: When marking complete, add brief notes on outcome or resolution for future reference
  • Age Monitoring: Review action items older than 30 days; consider breaking large tasks into smaller items

  • Decreasing Catastrophic/High Counts: Positive trend - mitigation efforts working; risks being downgraded or closed; continue current risk management practices

7.7 Risk Dashboard Best Practices

Effective Risk Dashboard Usage
  • Weekly Reviews: Review Risk Dashboard weekly to track emerging risks and mitigation progress; don't wait for monthly meetings
  • Drill-Down Habit: Don't just view summary cards - click into Risk Rating Matrix cells and Risk Details to understand specific risks
  • Trend Monitoring: Compare current period risk counts vs. previous periods to identify improving or degrading risk postures
  • Executive Reporting: Export Risk Dashboard visualizations (severity cards, risk matrix) for QBRs and executive presentations
  • Proactive Identification: Encourage teams to log risks early when they're easier to mitigate; reward proactive risk identification
  • Close the Loop: Ensure every risk has an owner, mitigation plan, and target date; track mitigation action items to completion
  • Learn from History: Review closed risks to identify patterns; document lessons learned; apply insights to prevent similar risks
  • Communicate Transparently: Share high-severity risks with customers proactively; transparency builds trust and enables collaborative mitigation
Risk vs. Issue Distinction
Risk: A potential future problem that hasn't occurred yet (e.g., "We might not find skilled resources in time").
Issue: An actual problem that has occurred and requires resolution (e.g., "Key resource resigned, causing project delay").

When a risk materializes (becomes reality), convert it from the Risk register to the Issues list and initiate resolution procedures. The system can maintain linkage between the original risk and resulting issue for traceability and lessons learned analysis.

Critical Survey Distribution Requirements
DO NOT skip CSM verification: Always send verification emails and wait for CSM approval before distributing surveys. Sending surveys to incorrect, outdated, or inappropriate contacts can:
  • Damage customer relationships (sending to departed employees, wrong stakeholders)
  • Violate privacy regulations (GDPR, CAN-SPAM)
  • Skew survey results (wrong respondent profile)
  • Reduce response rates (irrelevant recipients ignore surveys)
CSMs have the most current knowledge of customer contacts, project status, and relationship sensitivities. Their verification is mandatory.
Survey Response Rate Best Practices
To maximize survey response rates:
  • Personalize Invitations: Use respondent name, project/account name in email; avoid generic mass emails
  • Executive Sponsorship: Include message from senior leadership emphasizing importance of feedback
  • Optimal Send Times: Send Tuesday-Thursday, mid-morning (10-11 AM local time); avoid Mondays, Fridays, end-of-month
  • Survey Length: Keep surveys under 10 minutes; clearly state estimated completion time upfront
  • Timely Reminders: Send first reminder at Day 7; second reminder at Day 12; final reminder 2 days before close
  • Personal Follow-Up: For VIP accounts or low response, have CSM personally call/email key stakeholders
  • Incentive (optional): Offer drawing entry or small gift for completed surveys (check company policy)
  • Close the Loop: Commit to sharing survey results and action plans with respondents; demonstrate feedback drives change
  • Target 70-80% Response Rate: Industry standard for B2B relationship surveys; monitor batch summary table metrics
Survey Response Analysis
After survey close date:
  • Review Results: Access CSAT Insights dashboard (Section 6) to view NPS categories, analytics & trends, question-level responses
  • Identify At-Risk Accounts: Filter for Detractors (NPS 1-6) and low scores (ratings 1-3); prioritize for immediate follow-up
  • Automatic Action Items: System auto-creates action items for any survey scores < 4 (ratings 1, 2, or 3); assign to CSMs for remediation
  • Share Results with Teams: Export survey data, distribute to delivery teams, leadership; schedule review meetings
  • Develop Action Plans: For negative feedback, create specific improvement plans with owners, timelines, success metrics
  • Communicate Back to Customers: Share aggregate results (not individual responses); outline actions being taken based on feedback
  • Track Trends Over Time: Compare current batch results with previous cycles to measure improvement or identify declining satisfaction

10.12 Using Reports Effectively

Report Generation Best Practices
  • Define Date Ranges: Use StartDate and EndDate filters to focus on specific periods; avoid unnecessarily large date ranges that slow report generation
  • Filter by Customer: For account-specific analysis, select specific customer from dropdown instead of "All" to improve performance
  • Schedule Regular Reports: Generate key reports (CSS, CSAT, KPI Achievement) on consistent schedule (weekly/monthly/quarterly) for trend analysis
  • Export for Analysis: Export reports to Excel for deeper analysis, pivot tables, custom charts, and sharing with stakeholders
  • Compare Periods: Run same report for different time periods to identify trends, seasonal patterns, improvement/degradation
  • Cross-Reference Reports: Correlate data across reports (e.g., CSS scores with CSAT results, KPI achievement with process health)
  • Bookmark Frequent Reports: Save report URLs with pre-filled filters for one-click access to commonly used reports
  • Validate Data: If report results seem unexpected, verify underlying data in source modules before distributing
Data Refresh & Availability
Report data freshness:
  • Real-time reports: CSAT Survey Status, CSS Scores, Active Projects, Events/Tasks (refreshed on each report run)
  • Daily batch reports: KPI Actuals, Risk Repository, Issue Lists, Assessment Status (updated nightly at 12:00 AM UTC)
  • Weekly reports: Process Health Index, Delivery Leaderboard, Weekly Approvals (updated Sundays at 2:00 AM UTC)
  • Monthly reports: CSS Consolidated, Process Health by Audits, Training Compliance (updated 1st of month at 3:00 AM UTC)
Data retention: Report data is retained for 3 years; archived data older than 3 years can be requested from administrators.
Report Distribution & Confidentiality
Important: Reports may contain confidential customer data, internal metrics, and strategic information. Follow these guidelines:
  • Restrict Distribution: Share reports only with authorized stakeholders who have legitimate business need
  • Mask Sensitive Data: When sharing externally (with customers), remove internal columns (costs, internal notes, other customers)
  • Mark as Confidential: Label all exported reports as "Confidential" or "Internal Use Only" as appropriate
  • Secure Transmission: Use encrypted email or secure file sharing for distributing reports; avoid public cloud storage
  • Audit Trail: Report exports are logged with username, report type, filters, and timestamp for compliance auditing

Setting Effective Success Goals
  • SMART Goals: Ensure goals are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
  • Customer Alignment: Define success criteria jointly with customer; get written agreement
  • Balanced Scorecard: Include mix of delivery goals, quality goals, customer satisfaction, business value

Project vs. Account Navigation

Appendix B: Frequently Asked Questions

General Platform Questions

Q1: How do I reset my password?

A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. The link expires after 24 hours.

Q2: Can I access the platform from mobile devices?

A: Yes, the platform is responsive and works on tablets and smartphones. However, full functionality is best experienced on desktop browsers.

Q3: How often is dashboard data refreshed?

A: Dashboards auto-refresh every 5 minutes. Click the refresh icon to manually update data immediately.

Customer Management

Q4: How do I switch between multiple customers?

A: Use the customer selector dropdown in the top navigation bar. Your permissions determine which customers you can access.

Q5: Who can edit customer objectives?

A: CSMs, Customer Admins, and users with Admin role can edit customer objectives.

Tasks & Events

Q6: How do I create a recurring task?

A: When creating/editing a task, enable "Recurring Task" and configure the recurrence pattern (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).

Q7: Can I sync tasks with my Outlook calendar?

A: Yes, when you add participants to a task/event, .ics calendar invitations are sent that can be imported into Outlook or other calendar clients.

Ideas & Innovation (BVD)

Q8: Who can approve ideas?

A: Only users with "Approver" or "Admin" role can approve or reject ideas. Contact your administrator for permissions.

Q9: How is idea ROI calculated?

A: ROI = (Realized Value - Actual Cost) / Actual Cost × 100%

Customer Success Score (CSS)

Q10: How is the CSS score calculated?

A: CSS is a weighted composite of 6 factors: Service Level Achievement (25%), CSAT (25%), Project Health (20%), Engagement (15%), Risks (10%), Innovation (5%).

Q11: Can CSS weights be customized?

A: Yes, Admin users can configure custom weights and thresholds via the CSS Configuration panel.

Risk Management

Q12: What is a high-priority risk?

A: Risks with a score ≥13 (calculated as Likelihood × Impact) are considered high-priority and require escalation.

CSAT Surveys

Q13: How do I send a CSAT survey?

A: Navigate to PCSAT Configuration, create a survey cycle, add customer batches, verify details, then click "Send Survey Request".

Q14: Can I customize CSAT survey questions?

A: Custom questions can be configured by Admin users in the PCSAT Configuration module.

Reports & Exports

Q15: What export formats are available?

A: Most modules support export to PDF (visual reports) and Excel (detailed data).
