Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about findings, access, notifications, and project allocations.

Login Access

Who can access the CSM Platform?

Login Access

All employees have default login access to the CSM Platform. However, the projects visible to each employee depend on their active project allocations configured in D365 — employees will only see the projects they have been allocated to.

If you can log in but cannot see a specific project, check that your allocation for that project is active in D365. For allocation-related requests, contact WFM@neurealm.com.

Is CSM platform access available to contractors, or only employees?

Login Access

Contractors are granted default access to the CSM platform. Access is based on project allocation configured in D365 — contractors can view any project they are allocated to within the CSM platform.

Project Allocation Access

Why does a project not appear on my dashboard?

Project Allocation Access

Projects are displayed on your dashboard only when you have an active allocation for that project in D365. If your allocation has expired or not yet been created, the project will not be visible.

⚠️ If your allocation is expiring within 10 days, the platform will show an alert. Contact WFM@neurealm.com to request an extension before it expires.

How do I extend or add access to a project in the CSM platform?

Project Allocation Access

Project allocations and extensions must be managed within D365. Once updated in D365, the data will automatically synchronize with the CSM platform — no separate action is needed in CSM.

To request a new allocation or extension in D365, contact the WFM team at WFM@neurealm.com.

A user cannot see their other projects when logging a risk. How is this fixed?

Project Allocation Access

Risks can only be logged for projects with a current active allocation. If a user cannot see certain projects, their allocation in D365 must be updated. Changes made in D365 will synchronize automatically with the CSM portal.

To update project allocations in D365, contact the WFM team at WFM@neurealm.com.

An allocation was revised in D365 but the project still doesn't appear in the CSM portal. What next?

Project Allocation Access

If the allocation has been extended in D365 but is still not visible in the CSM portal, there may be a data discrepancy. To resolve this:

  • 1Take a screenshot of the D365 allocation showing the revised/extended dates.
  • 2Contact the WFM team at WFM@neurealm.com to verify the D365 allocation update.
  • 3If the issue persists in the CSM portal after D365 is updated, forward the request with the screenshot to csmplatformsupport@neurealm.com for a discrepancy review.

Findings Closure Process

What is the full finding closure process?

Findings Closure Process

After the Auditor creates a finding, the Auditee must accept or reject it. The path differs based on that decision:

  • ①Auditee Rejects: The Auditor is notified and can either accept the rejection (status → Rejection-Complete) or revert the finding back to step one.
  • ②Auditee Accepts: The finding proceeds through four CAPA stages (see below).

Evidence is mandatory in either Stage 3 (Implementation) or Stage 4 (Verification). Duplicate evidence cannot be uploaded for the same finding.

What are the four CAPA stages an accepted finding must go through?

Findings Closure Process
  • 1CAP Submission (Auditee): Submit a plan covering Potential Cause, Root Cause (Yes/No), Correction (immediate fix), Corrective Action Plan, Target Date, Responsibility, and Plan for Verifying Effectiveness.
  • 2CAP Approval (Auditor): Auditor reviews and approves the submitted corrective action plan, optionally adding remarks.
  • 3CAP Implementation (Auditee): Auditee selects the IsImplemented status (Yes/No) and provides remarks. Evidence is mandatory here or in Stage 4.
  • 4CAP Verification (Auditor): Auditor selects IsCompleted/Closed (Yes/No). If No, they choose to Re-submit CAP (back to Stage 1) or Re-implement CAP (back to Stage 3).

What do the coloured stage indicators on each finding mean?

Findings Closure Process

Each finding shows four numbered stage circles that act as a RAG status indicator for the CAPA workflow:

Colour Meaning When it applies
Red Pending / Not yet completed Default state for all 4 stages. Remains red when the Auditee first accepts the finding and CAPA has not yet started.
Green Completed A stage turns green when successfully completed. All 4 circles are green when the finding is fully closed through CAPA.
Grey Not applicable (Rejection-Complete) All 4 circles turn grey when the Auditee rejects the finding and the Auditor accepts the rejection — status becomes Rejection-Complete. No CAPA is required.

Example: If Stage 1 and Stage 2 are done but Stage 3 is pending, you will see 🟢 → 🟢 → 🔴 → 🔴

Why is Stage 2 (CAP Approval) locked?

Findings Closure Process

Stage 2 (CAP Approval) is only unlocked after the Auditee successfully submits Stage 1 (CAP Submission). Until Stage 1 is submitted, the Auditor cannot proceed with the review.

The Auditee must expand the accepted finding and click "Add Correction Plan" to begin Stage 1.

Why is Stage 3 (CAP Implementation) locked?

Findings Closure Process

Stage 3 (CAP Implementation) unlocks only after the Auditor approves Stage 2 with the status "Corrective Action Plan Approved". If Stage 2 is still pending or rejected, Stage 3 remains locked.

Why is Stage 4 (CAP Verification) locked?

Findings Closure Process

Stage 4 (CAP Verification) unlocks only after the Auditee submits Stage 3 with the status "Corrective Action Plan Implemented". The Auditor can then verify and close the finding.

What happens if Stage 4 (CAP Verification) is not approved?

Findings Closure Process

If the Auditor selects "No" for Is Completed/Closed in Stage 4, they must choose a recommended action:

  • 1Re-Submit CAP — Sends the finding back to Stage 1 (CAP Submission) for the Auditee to revise the corrective action plan.
  • 2Re-Implement CAP — Sends the finding back to Stage 3 (CAP Implementation) for the Auditee to re-implement and provide updated evidence.

Findings Closure Notifications

Why can't I access Audit Findings from the notification email link?

Findings Closure Notifications

Access to view assessments and findings requires a current project allocation, even if the user is listed as the Auditee. Please confirm that the user has an active allocation for the project in question.

Being named as the Auditee does not automatically grant access — the project allocation in D365 must be current and active.

Why am I receiving repeated automated emails about open findings assessment?

Findings Closure Notifications

These daily Open Findings Assessment emails are not a technical error. They are triggered because certain findings still require action. There are two main causes:

  • 1Accepted findings: Some findings have been accepted but the Corrective Action Plan (CAPA) stages have not been completed.
  • 2Pending decisions: Other findings have not yet been accepted or rejected, so they cannot progress.

Completing the required CAPA stages or accepting/rejecting the pending findings will stop the automated notifications.

Roles & Responsibilities

How do I know my CSM Role?

Roles & Responsibilities

Your CSM Role is shown at the top-right of every page as a small tag next to your name — for example, Your Name Project Manager. Hover over the tag to see "Your Role in CSM Platform".

  • 1Note the role in the tag next to your name.
  • 2Open "What can my role see and do?" below. It opens on your own role automatically and shows your menus and permissions.

Role looks wrong? Email csmplatformsupport@neurealm.com with your Email ID, the role shown, and the role you expect. After the role is updated, log out and log back in to see the new menus.

I'm new to CSM — how does access work, and where are things on screen?

Roles & Responsibilities

Two separate things decide what you see after you log in:

Your CSM Role

Shown top-right as a tag next to your name. Decides which menus you see and whether you can view, create, edit, or delete in them.

Your project link (from D365)

Synced automatically from D365. Decides which projects and customer accounts appear inside those menus. A project appears if you are either:

  • Allocated to the project, or
  • Assigned to the project as its PM, BU Head, CSM, or DP.

Added in D365 = appears in CSM; removed = disappears.

Can't see a project? You are probably neither allocated to it nor assigned as its PM, BU Head, CSM, or DP in D365. That's a D365 data gap, not a CSM permission issue. Contact WFM@neurealm.com first.
