Consequences of the event on CIA (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)
Guideline in qualitative words
None 0 No consequences — for example the control has eliminated them.
Insignificant 1 No visible impact to company reputation/customer satisfaction. No potential impact on market share/brand values.
Minor 2 Potential impact on market share/brand values. Internal control significant deficiency.
Significant 3 Visible reputation/satisfaction impact. Reputation and brand value will be affected in the short term. Internal control material weakness.
Major 4 Visible adverse brand value/market share publicity. Key alliances are threatened. Loss of key customers. SEC investigation or matter. Financial restatement.
Critical 5 Major company reputation impact. Revocation of licenses or regulatory registrations. Major customer satisfaction impact. Inability to service customers. Loss of major investor/street confidence.
Guideline to articulate a risk statement :-
Template

Event that has an effect on <objectives/goals> caused by <cause(s)> may result in <Consequence(s)>

For example :-

Confidential data leakage or unavailability caused by poor patch management and anti-virus software update may result in financial fraud loss of $$ million and fine from HIPAA $$$ value, loss of customer confidence in Healthcare sector resulting in net loss of revenue $$ %

Explanation :-

Confidential data leakage, unavailability are information security failure events; keeping information secure (the objective) has deviated from (the effect). Poor patch management or AV update are the causes of this effect on objectives, while the consequences are articulated in terms of what happens if the risk occurs — in other words if the project / organization fails to meet its objective.
