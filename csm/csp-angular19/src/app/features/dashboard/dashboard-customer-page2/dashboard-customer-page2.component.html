Quality

To measure the outcome of service & delivery from Quality aspect (how free it is of errors).

Example :-

1) Percentage of User stories accepted without defect in every release

2) Percentage of Incidents / Service requests re-opened by user

3) Percentage / No of defects delivered per user story
Performance

To measure how fast or slow is service / delivery.

Example :-

1) Percentage of incidents responded & resolved within agreed SLA

2) Percentage of User stories accepted as committed

3) Percentage of projects / initiatives progressing / completed within timeline
Value

To measure service & delivery on business outcome achieved, customer’s preferences and customer’s perception.

Example :-

1) Business outcome achieved – Cost – saved / avoided

2) User satisfaction after implementing project(s) in live / production

3) Customer Satisfaction survey feedback 4. Percentage of incidents resolved prior to business impact
Complaince

To measure if the service delivery comply as per compliance requirements, policies, procedures & standards

Example :-

1) Percentage of Services complied as per contractual requirements including Security, Clean room requirements.

2) Percentage of User stories delivered as per quality & security standards
